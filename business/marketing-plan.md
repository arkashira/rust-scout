# marketing-plan.md

## Positioning
**Primary Positioning Statement:**  
"Rust-Scout: Your curated platform for discovering and managing star-trending Rust libraries, streamlining your project development."

**Alternative Positioning Statements:**  
1. "Unlock the potential of Rust with Rust-Scout: the ultimate resource for trending libraries tailored for developers."
2. "Stay ahead in Rust development with Rust-Scout: the go-to platform for efficient library management."
3. "Elevate your Rust projects with Rust-Scout: discover and manage the best libraries effortlessly."

## Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
1. **Persona 1: Alex the Rust Developer**  
   - **Daily Job:** Building and maintaining web applications using Rust.  
   - **Budget:** $50/month for development tools and resources.  

2. **Persona 2: Jamie the Software Engineer**  
   - **Daily Job:** Collaborating on open-source Rust projects and seeking efficient libraries for integration.  
   - **Budget:** $75/month for tools that enhance productivity and collaboration.  

3. **Persona 3: Taylor the Tech Lead**  
   - **Daily Job:** Overseeing a team of developers and ensuring the use of best practices in Rust development.  
   - **Budget:** $100/month for team resources and training materials.

## Channels
1. **Developer Forums & Communities (e.g., Reddit, Stack Overflow)**  
   - **CAC Estimate:** $20 per acquisition through targeted ads and community engagement.

2. **Content Marketing (Blog Posts, Tutorials)**  
   - **CAC Estimate:** $15 per acquisition through organic traffic and SEO optimization.

3. **Social Media Advertising (Twitter, LinkedIn)**  
   - **CAC Estimate:** $30 per acquisition through targeted ads focusing on Rust developers.

## Content Cadence
- **Week 1:** Launch blog post introducing Rust-Scout and its benefits.  
- **Week 2:** Publish a tutorial on how to effectively use Rust-Scout for project development.  
- **Week 3:** Share case studies of successful Rust projects utilizing trending libraries.  
- **Week 4:** Host a webinar on Rust development best practices featuring Rust-Scout.  
- **Week 5:** Release a comparison guide of popular Rust libraries available on Rust-Scout.  
- **Week 6:** Engage with the community through Q&A sessions on social media.  
- **Week 7:** Publish user testimonials and success stories.  
- **Week 8:** Share insights on Rust trends and future developments.  
- **Week 9:** Conduct a survey for user feedback and feature requests.  
- **Week 10:** Announce new features and updates based on user feedback.  
- **Week 11:** Create a video series on using Rust-Scout effectively.  
- **Week 12:** Recap the journey and share future plans for Rust-Scout.

## Launch Milestones
- **D-30:** Finalize product features and prepare marketing materials.  
- **D-0:** Official launch of Rust-Scout with promotional campaigns across channels.  
- **D+30:** Analyze user feedback and engagement metrics; iterate on marketing strategies.  
- **D+90:** Review overall performance, adjust product features based on user needs, and plan for future enhancements.

## Success Metrics
- **Daily Active Users (DAU):** Target 1,000 DAU by D+90.  
- **Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) Target:** Achieve $5,000 MRR by D+90.
