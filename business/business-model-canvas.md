# Business Model Canvas for rust-scout

## Customer Segments
- Rust developers seeking efficient library management solutions.
- Software development teams looking to streamline project development with curated libraries.
- Open-source contributors and maintainers interested in promoting their libraries.

## Value Propositions
- Curated platform that simplifies the discovery of star-trending Rust libraries.
- Enhanced project efficiency through easy access to high-quality, vetted libraries.
- Community-driven insights and ratings to guide library selection.

## Channels
- Direct access via the rust-scout website and GitHub repository.
- Social media platforms (Twitter, Reddit) for community engagement and updates.
- Developer forums and Rust community events for outreach and feedback.

## Customer Relationships
- Community engagement through forums and user feedback loops.
- Regular updates and newsletters to keep users informed about new libraries and features.
- Support via GitHub issues and community channels for troubleshooting and suggestions.

## Revenue Streams
- Subscription model for premium features (estimated $10/month or ~350 THB).
- Affiliate partnerships with library maintainers for featured listings (potential revenue share).
- Sponsored content or advertisements from relevant tech companies.

## Key Resources
- Development team skilled in Rust and web technologies.
- Community of Rust developers and contributors for continuous feedback and improvement.
- Access to datasets for library performance and usage trends.

## Key Activities
- Continuous curation and updating of Rust libraries on the platform.
- Development and maintenance of the rust-scout platform and user interface.
- Marketing efforts to grow user base and community engagement.

## Key Partners
- Rust community organizations and forums for collaboration and outreach.
- Library maintainers for featured listings and partnerships.
- Tech companies interested in advertising or sponsorship opportunities.

## Cost Structure
- Development and operational costs (estimated $5,000/month or ~175,000 THB).
- Marketing and community engagement expenses (estimated $1,000/month or ~35,000 THB).
- Hosting and infrastructure costs for the platform (estimated $500/month or ~17,500 THB).
